Part 1: Logic Check — Does the Second Sentence Explain the First?
Directions: Read each pair of sentences. Decide whether the second sentence (B) logically

  ✓  Write  Y  if Sentence B explains Sentence A.
  ✗  Write  N  if Sentence B does NOT explain Sentence A.
⏱  Time limit: 30 seconds per question.
Answer Key → write your answer in the blank before checking.

Part 2: Keyword Expansion — Which Detail Best Develops the Claim?
Directions: Each question provides a CLAIM sentence. Four options each pick a keyword from
the claim and suggest an expansion direction.
Choose the option that provides the MOST specific and logical explanation.
⏱  Time limit: 45 seconds per question.
Key insight: The best answer turns an ABSTRACT word into something SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, or CAUSAL.
Q01. I think long commuting hours will bring lower sense of happiness for people.
A. "long hours" → specify exactly how many hours count as "long" (e.g., over 2 hours per day), using statistical data to show this is abnormal.
B. "commuting" → explain the history of how commuting became common in modern cities.
C. "lower sense of happiness" → list specific negative consequences such as chronic fatigue, depressed mood, and disrupted family time.
D. "people" → describe the different types of people who commute by bus, train, or car.
Your answer: ___________
Answer: C
【分析】选C最直接：通过列举"不快乐"的具体表现（疲劳、抑郁情绪、家庭时间减少），把抽象的"lower sense of happiness"变成可感知的细节。选A也可接受（量化长时间），但C的展开维度更丰富。
Q02. Students who spend too much time on social media tend to perform worse academically.
A. "students" → describe the age range and demographic background of students who use social media most.
B. "too much time" → define a specific threshold, such as more than 3 hours per day, and cite research showing its correlation with GPA decline.
C. "social media" → list the most popular platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat.
D. "perform worse" → explain that grades are measured differently in different countries.
Your answer: ___________
Answer: B
【分析】选B最有说服力：通过具体数字（每天超3小时）量化"too much"，并用GPA数据建立直接因果证据链。
Q03. Living in a city with high air pollution can cause serious damage to children's lung development.
A. "city" → describe the population size and infrastructure of major polluted cities worldwide.
B. "high air pollution" → specify PM2.5 concentration levels that are considered dangerous (e.g., above 75 μg/m³) and name cities that regularly exceed this.
C. "serious damage" → explain that adults are also affected by pollution, not just children.
D. "children" → mention that children under the age of 18 are still in school.
Your answer: ___________
Answer: B
【分析】选B通过具体的PM2.5数值量化"高污染"，并提供真实参照城市，使观点从抽象变为可验证的事实。
Q04. Teenagers who lack financial education are more likely to fall into debt in their early adulthood.
A. "teenagers" → mention that many teenagers are too young to open a bank account.
B. "lack financial education" → give examples of what missing knowledge looks like: not understanding interest rates, credit card fees, or the risk of impulse buying.

D. "early adulthood" → define the age range of 18–25 as a transitional period in life.

Answer: B
【分析】选B通过列举缺乏金融教育的具体表现（不懂利率、信用卡费、冲动消费），让"缺乏教育"变得具体而非空洞。

Part 3: Sentence Extension — Expand with If / When / Unless / Since
【常用扩展连接词速查】

• especially when ... (as / since ...)  →  最常用的"场景+原因"双层扩展
• if + 条件 → 结果                      →  假设情境扩展
• unless + 否定条件                     →  反向推理
• once + 完成条件 → 结果                →  达成某条件后的影响
• particularly when / in cases where    →  特定情境聚焦
• as long as                            →  持续条件

Directions: Each question provides a SHORT claim. Four options expand the claim differently.
Choose the option with the RICHEST logical extension — specific scenario + consequence/explanation.
⏱  Time limit: 45 seconds per question.
Key insight: The best answer adds a SPECIFIC CONDITION (when/if) + EXPLAINS the MECHANISM (as/since/because).
Q01. Base claim: "Long commuting hours reduce happiness."
A. Long commuting hours reduce happiness, and many people commute every day.
B. Long commuting hours reduce happiness, because cities are getting bigger.
C. Long commuting hours reduce happiness, especially when people spend more than two hours stuck in traffic each day, as this leaves them with almost no time to exercise, cook a proper meal, or simply rest before returning to work the next morning.
D. Long commuting hours reduce happiness, so governments should build better public transport.
Your answer: ___________
Answer: C
【分析】选C用"especially when"引入具体场景（每天超2小时堵在路上），再用"as this leaves them"连接具体后果（无时间运动/做饭/休息），句子层次丰富，逻辑完整。

A. Social media affects teenagers' self-esteem, and it is very popular among young people.
B. Social media negatively affects teenagers' self-esteem, particularly when they constantly compare their appearance or achievements to the carefully filtered highlight reels of their peers, because this comparison triggers feelings of inadequacy that can persist long after they put down their phones.
C. Social media affects teenagers' self-esteem, so parents should set time limits.
D. Social media affects teenagers' self-esteem, which is a serious problem in modern society.
Your answer: ___________
Answer: B
【分析】选B用"particularly when"引入具体场景（比较经过滤镜处理的他人生活），再用"because"解释心理机制（比较→自我怀疑→持续影响），展开逻辑严密。

Quick Review: The 3 Reasoning Rules
Rule 1 — Logic Check
Every sentence you add must EXPLAIN the sentence before it.
Ask yourself: "Does this sentence answer WHY or HOW the previous statement is true?"
If not — cut it or replace it.
Rule 2 — Keyword Expansion
Find the MOST ABSTRACT word in your claim. That is your target.
Make it SPECIFIC: add numbers, name categories, describe consequences.
Abstract → Concrete: 'unhappy' → 'depressed, can't sleep, no energy to exercise'
Rule 3 — Sentence Extension
Use: especially when ... (as/since ...) to add BOTH a scenario AND a mechanism.
Short claim + 'especially when [specific condition]' + 'as/since [reason]' = full reasoning.
Practice: take any 5-word opinion → build it into 2 full complex sentences.
