# Example 趣味练习

按天解锁：完成「第 N 天」全部选择题后，下次进入会解锁第 N+1 天。内容使用与语法材料相同的「级别1：选择题」格式。

---

## Day 1

**级别1：选择题**
```
1. She _____ to school every morning.
A. go      B. goes      C. going

2. They _____ English very well.
A. speaks      B. speak      C. speaking

答案：1-B, 2-B
```

---

## Day 2

**级别1：选择题**
```
1. If it rains tomorrow, we _____ at home.
A. stay      B. stays      C. stayed

2. I wish I _____ more time to read.
A. have      B. had      C. has

答案：1-A, 2-B
```

---

## Day 3

**级别1：选择题**
```
1. The book _____ by many students last year.
A. read      B. was read      C. reads

2. By next year, she _____ here for ten years.
A. will live      B. will have lived      C. lives

答案：1-B, 2-B
```
